# MISSION: HIGH-CONVERSION EXPORT PORTAL FOR AGR APPARELS

## 1. BRAND CONTEXT
AGR Apparels is a Tiruppur-based manufacturing giant established in 1990, exporting 75% of its volume to Europe. The website must look like a premium European B2B fashion partner.

## 2. KEY SECTIONS & CONTENT
- **HERO:** Headline: "Three Decades of Textile Excellence. Exported to the World." Sub-headline: "Tiruppur's premier garment manufacturer for Men, Women, and Kids. Serving the European market since 1990."
- **THE EXPORT MAP:** An interactive SVG map highlighting their 75% export reach into Europe.
- **PRODUCT BENTO:** Use a 21st.dev style Bento Grid. 
  - Large Tile: Men’s Casual & Active (Hoodies/Sandos).
  - Medium Tile: Ladies’ Trend Collection (V-Nicks/Spaghetti Tops).
  - Square Tiles: Kids’ Fancy wear and Baby Rompers.
- **QUALITY PILLARS:** High-tech icons for "Skin-Friendly Fabrics," "Custom Packaging," and "Garanted Delivery."
- **LEGACY TIMELINE:** A scroll-triggered timeline starting from 1990 to the present.

## 3. ANIMATION & TRANSITION (MUST-HAVES)
- **Smooth Inertial Scroll:** Use Lenis for a luxurious feel.
- **Image Reveal:** Horizontal "Curtain Wipe" animations for product images to mimic fabric unfolding.
- **Parallax Layers:** Subtle parallax on high-res factory floor shots in the background.
- **Component Request:** Use "Animated Testimonial Cards" and "Infinite Certification Marquee" from 21st.dev.

## 4. TECHNICAL STACK
Next.js, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, Shadcn/UI.
